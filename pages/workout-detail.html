Foundations: Push–Pull Reset

Six movements that rebuild honest strength in the shoulders, back, and core before anything fancier. Run this three times a week for four weeks before moving to the Skill Ladder.

25 minDuration
6Exercises
NoneEquipment
3×/weekFrequency
The routine

Six moves, three rounds, full attention to tempo.

Rest 60–75 seconds between exercises. Move to round two only after all six are done once.

01

Incline Pike Push-Up

Hands on the floor, hips high, feet elevated on a step. Lower the crown of your head toward your hands.

3 × 8Sets × Reps
02

Scapular Pull-Ups

Dead hang on a bar, elbows straight, pull the shoulder blades down and together without bending the arms.

3 × 6Sets × Reps
03

Tempo Bodyweight Squat

4-second descent, 1-second pause at depth, explode up. Knees track over the toes throughout.

3 × 10Sets × Reps
04

Ring Rows (or Table Rows)

Body straight, chest to hands at the top. Adjust foot position to change difficulty — feet closer is harder.

3 × 10Sets × Reps
05

Hollow Body Hold

Lower back pressed flat, ribs down, arms and legs extended. Breathe without letting the back arch off the floor.

3 × 20sSets × Time
06

Wall-Assisted Handstand Hold

Chest to the wall, stack shoulders over wrists, squeeze the whole body tight. Step down before form breaks.

3 × 15sSets × Time
Coaching note — if the pike push-up feels too easy by week two, raise your feet another few centimetres rather than adding reps past 10. Range of motion builds the shoulder strength this routine is actually chasing.
